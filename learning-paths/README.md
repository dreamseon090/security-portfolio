## 2025-08 → 2025-12 · Foundations

I started with no formal IT background. Alongside school, I spent five months building
the groundwork through two Korean national IT qualifications — programming logic,
databases, operating systems, networking, and server administration.

Both were passed on the first attempt in December 2025.

- [Craftsman Information Processing](../certifications/01-craftsman-information-processing/) — HRD Korea
- [Network Administrator Level 2](../certifications/02-network-administrator/) — ICQA

This phase was deliberately unglamorous. It gave me the vocabulary and the mental
models — how a query is parsed, how a permission model works, how a packet moves —
that everything after it depends on.

---

## 2025-12 → 2026-06 · TryHackMe

This is where I moved from studying computers to attacking them.

I followed the TryHackMe roadmap end to end, completing six learning paths from
Pre Security through Red Teaming, alongside a large volume of CTF practice. By July
2026 I had reached the top 1% of users globally, with a peak rank of #13 in South
Korea.

[Full breakdown of the paths →](TryHackMe/)

Working through a structured roadmap rather than picking rooms at random mattered
here. Each path assumed the one before it, so gaps in my understanding surfaced
immediately instead of quietly persisting.
